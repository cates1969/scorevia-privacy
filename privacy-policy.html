Privacy Policy — ScoreVia

Last updated: May 1, 2026

Carlos Andres Sierra ("we", "our") operates the ScoreVia mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: name, email address, and country of residence upon registration.
  • Usage information: match predictions, accumulated points, and tournament participation.
  • Payment information: payments are processed through Stripe and PayPal. We do not store credit card data.
  • Push notification token: to send you tournament-related alerts.
  • Device information: device type and operating system to improve compatibility.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the sports prediction service.
  • Manage your account and tournament registrations.
  • Process payments and prize distributions.
  • Send push notifications related to tournaments.
  • Display rankings and statistics within the application.
  • Improve our application and detect technical issues.

3. Sharing Information with Third Parties

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Stripe: to process credit card payments.
  • PayPal: to process payments and withdrawals.
  • Firebase (Google): for authentication, database, and push notifications.
  • Expo: for push notification delivery.

4. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us. We use Firebase Authentication to protect your account and HTTPS for all communications. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information while you maintain an active account in ScoreVia. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us.

6. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Object to the processing of your data.

7. Children's Privacy

ScoreVia is not directed to persons under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
